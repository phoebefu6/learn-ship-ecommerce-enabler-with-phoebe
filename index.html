Ship It: Social Commerce Enablers

A social-commerce enabler runs a brand's stores, content, livestreams, ads and customer service across several marketplaces, and owns neither the goods nor the buyer. That one fact decides its data roadmap. Its margin is the hours it stops spending, so the first use cases to build are the ones that remove work, and the pricing optimisation most roadmaps start with mostly pays the brand. Its moat is real at the store and the SKU, where the keys are its own, and does not exist at the customer, where the marketplaces mask the buyer. Six leader sessions follow Serai Commerce, a fictional enabler, from its money map to a metric registry fit for a listing. Eight analyst sessions build the estate on real Olist orders and end on a bench that measures, pair by pair, which joins survive the masking.
