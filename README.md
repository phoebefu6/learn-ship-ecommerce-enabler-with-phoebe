# Ship It: Social Commerce Enablers

Fourteen 45-minute sessions on the data roadmap of a social-commerce enabler: a company that runs
brands' stores, content, livestreams, ads and customer service across marketplaces and owns
neither the goods nor the buyer.

- **Leader track (6, no code):** what an enabler sells, why its margin is hours removed, a new
  live channel taken from GMV-only to a scored funnel, what the marketplaces let you keep, three
  products from one estate, and the metric registry before a listing.
- **Analyst track (8, Python and SQL):** seven data domains, three marketplace exports conformed to
  one schema, the identity gate under masking, per-minute live events, stage metrics as code,
  content to creator yield, the metric registry as code, and the enabler bench.

The bench (analyst session 8) runs in the browser on 8,614 real orders from the public Olist
dataset (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), split into three constructed marketplace-style exports. It scores any
choice of join keys against the hidden real customer, store and SKU ids, and sequences ten use
cases from one editable assumptions block. `materials/build-enabler-bench.py` is the Python
reference that prints the same numbers.

Every company on these pages is fictional. Serai Commerce, Pasar Live and every figure about them
are constructed and labelled so.

Open `index.html`, or start at `courses/a1-what-an-enabler-sells.html` (leader) or
`courses/b1-seven-domains.html` (analyst). By Phoebe Fu.
